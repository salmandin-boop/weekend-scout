Weekend Scout

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Weekend Scout (the "app") is run by ENDins LLC, a New York limited liability company based in Utica, NY ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short, we don't collect personal information.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no accounts, sign-ups, ad networks or advertising trackers. We use privacy-friendly visit counting (see below) to see how many people use the app and which pages they open. Some event listings are paid placements from venues; they're labeled Sponsored and don't use any information about you.

What stays on your device

When you save an event, the app remembers it in your phone or browser's own storage so your Saved list and the city you picked are there next time. It also keeps a copy of the current event list so the app works offline. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Your location

If you tap "Use my location" or "Nearest first", your phone shares its approximate location with the app while it is open, so it can find the closest of our cities and show how far each event is. That check happens on your device. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is not saved.

"Picked for you"

The app counts which kinds of events you open and save (for example, music or comedy) so it can suggest similar events. Those counts are kept in your phone or browser's storage only and never leave your device. Clearing the app's data erases them.

Services the app connects to

  • Hosting: the app and its weekly event list are served from a web host (GitHub Pages). Like any website, the host may briefly log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure.
  • Visit counting: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages and cities are popular. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other websites and doesn't build a profile of you. Cloudflare receives technical details of each visit (such as the page, your browser type, country and the site that sent you) under its privacy policy.
  • Fonts: the app's typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them.
  • Links you tap: ticket sellers, venue websites, Google Maps and Google Calendar open outside the app and follow their own privacy policies. Some ticket links are affiliate links, which means the ticket site may know you came from us.

If you contact us

If you email or text us, for example to list an event, we use your message and contact details only to reply and handle your request. We don't sell or share them.

Children

The app is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
