To keep Weekend Scout safe, we check every business before it goes live:

  1. It's registered with the state and listed on Google Maps at the same address.
  2. We call back on its public phone number.
  3. The event is on its official website or a major ticket seller.
  4. Featured spots are limited to 3 per city each weekend, first approved, first booked. After approval we send a secure card payment link (Stripe) in the business's name. If a weekend is full, we offer the next open one.

Good to know

  • Times and prices can change. Check with the venue before you go.
  • Some ticket links may earn us a small commission, at no cost to you.
  • Featured events are paid placements and are always labeled Sponsored.
  • Your saved events stay on this device only. We don't collect any personal data.
  • "Use my location" and "Nearest first" work out distances on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or stored.
  • "Picked for you" learns from what you open and save, on this phone only.
  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo and the map from OpenStreetMap. Neither gets your location.
